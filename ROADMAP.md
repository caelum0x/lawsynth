# Roadmap

The present priority is to harden the implemented local engine: World IR,
bundle integrity, numerical simulation, reproducible discovery, and bindings.

Future architectural areas—causal analysis, regime inference, uncertainty
quantification, plugins, Studio, services, and deployment—remain proposals
until their source, tests, and user-facing contracts are implemented. Their
presence in the architecture and manifest is planning information, not a
release commitment or availability statement.

Priorities are decided through maintainership review and reproducible technical
evidence rather than calendar promises.
