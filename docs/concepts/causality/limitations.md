# Limits of the causal utilities

Unmeasured confounding, contemporaneous feedback, selection bias, measurement error, and nonstationary sampling can make predictive associations misleading. `e_value` provides a sensitivity quantity from a positive finite risk ratio; it does not repair any of those problems.

The package does not fit probabilistic graphical models or learn causal structure. Its safe role is to make declared structure, ordering, and predictive diagnostics inspectable.
