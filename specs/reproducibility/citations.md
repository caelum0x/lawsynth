# Citations and provenance

World IR and format-0.1 bundles have no citation, author, institution,
license, dataset-source, or method-reference fields. The code does not collect
or validate bibliographic metadata.

Research owners should retain citations in a versioned external record bound to
the input digest, plan description, environment record, and output artifact.
Citation metadata must be reviewed by the owner; this engine cannot verify the
truth, license, or continued availability of external references.
